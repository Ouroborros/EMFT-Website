About EMFT

EMFT — Emerging Market Financial Training — exists to close the skills gap in emerging-market finance.

Our mission

Training our clients can rely on

Our mission is simple: to deliver financial training that our clients can rely on — honestly scoped, professionally taught, and reinforced long after the last session.

EMFT is a team of finance practitioners and educators who believe that expertise means little until it transfers. We combine hands-on emerging-market experience with disciplined teaching, serving banks, regulators, and investment firms that need their people ready for real decisions. What unites us is a shared standard: if it carries our name, it's taught properly.

At a glance

  • Practitioner-led

    Every course taught by people who have done the job.

  • Market-specific

    Cases and examples from the markets you operate in.

  • Reinforced after delivery

    The last session is the start of the relationship, not the end.

Core values

What we stand for

Integrity

We give honest assessments and keep our commitments, even when the easier answer would win the work.

Quality

We hold every deliverable to a standard we'd stake our reputation on, because we do.

Clarity

We communicate in plain language: clear scopes, clear pricing, clear expectations.

Partnership

We measure success by what our participants can do afterwards, not by seats filled.

Our approach

Three steps, no shortcuts

  1. 1

    Understand

    Every engagement begins with listening. We take the time to understand your people, goals, and context before proposing anything — because the right program depends entirely on the real skills gap.

  2. 2

    Deliver

    We commit to clear learning outcomes, then deliver with discipline. You get a defined syllabus, practitioner instructors, and a single accountable team from start to finish.

  3. 3

    Support

    The course ending isn't goodbye. We stay available with follow-up sessions, materials, and answers — so what your team learned actually sticks.

Work with us

Tell us what your people need to learn. We'll come back with a straight answer about how we can help — and whether we're the right fit.
