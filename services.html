Services

What we do

Financial skills decide outcomes — a credit decision made well, a risk caught early, a portfolio positioned right. EMFT builds those skills across emerging markets through four core services, each delivered by the same standard: understand the need first, commit to clear learning outcomes, and see them through.

Our services

Corporate Training Programs

The strongest programs are built, not bought. EMFT designs in-house training around your institution's needs — from credit and risk to treasury and compliance — and delivers it on-site or virtually, using case studies drawn from the markets you operate in.

What's included

  • Training-needs analysis and tailored curriculum design
  • On-site or virtual delivery, led by experienced practitioners
  • Case studies and exercises drawn from your own markets
  • Post-program assessment, materials, and follow-up sessions

Open Courses & Workshops

Our scheduled open courses give individual professionals and small teams access to the same practitioner-led training our institutional clients rely on. Cohorts are kept small, sessions are hands-on, and every course ends with materials you'll actually use again.

What's included

  • Core topics: credit analysis, risk management, treasury, capital markets, and compliance
  • Small cohorts with hands-on exercises and discussion
  • Certificates of completion for participants
  • Course materials and reference guides to keep

Advisory & Capability Building

Sometimes the answer isn't a course — it's a plan. We help institutions understand where their skills gaps are and build the internal capability to close them, from curriculum design through to training their own trainers.

What's included

  • Skills-gap and training-needs assessments
  • Curriculum and learning-pathway design
  • In-house academy setup and train-the-trainer programs
  • Ongoing capability roadmaps and periodic reviews

E-Learning & Blended Programs

When teams are distributed or time is short, learning has to flex. Our online modules and blended programs combine self-paced study with live virtual workshops — so training fits the calendar instead of fighting it.

What's included

  • Self-paced online modules on core finance topics
  • Live virtual workshops and Q&A sessions
  • Blended learning paths combining online and in-person delivery
  • Progress tracking and completion reporting for managers

Not sure which format fits your team?

A short conversation is usually enough to point you in the right direction.
