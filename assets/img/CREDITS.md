Note on `program-cyber.jpg`: the rawpixel record titles it "Officers U.S.
Customs Border Protection". The frame used shows analysts at workstations
with wall displays and carries no visible insignia or branding, and the
image is public domain, but the provenance is noted here in case you would
rather not use a government-facility photograph.

Each was cropped centre-weighted to 3:2 at 960x640 and re-encoded by
`scripts/add-program-photos.py`, which also rejects anything too dark or too
washed out to carry a card title.
