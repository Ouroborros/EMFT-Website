Technical & Custom Assessments

Measure before you invest.

Objective, practitioner-built assessments in the two domains we know best — finance and emerging technology. Every assessment is customized to your roles, competencies and market — never off the shelf.

Domain one

Financial Assessments

For banks, central banks and sovereign wealth funds — the core of what EMFT does.

  • Credit and financial analysis
  • Investment analysis, portfolio construction and valuation
  • Risk, treasury and asset-liability management
  • Banking supervision and inspection readiness
  • Qualification readiness — CISI, CFA and regulatory exams
Domain two

Emerging Tech Assessments

For institutions building tomorrow's technical capability inside today's teams.

  • Cybersecurity — defensive (blue team) and offensive (penetration testing)
  • Data, analytics and AI
  • Digital transformation, fintech and open banking
  • GenAI, agentic AI and prompt engineering
  • Power BI and data visualization
  • Blockchain, digital assets and distributed ledger
Built for you

Every assessment is customized.

We do not sell a fixed test. Question banks, case studies, simulations and scoring rubrics are written around your job descriptions, your products and your market — then calibrated with practitioners from your domain.

  • Built from your job descriptions, competency framework and policies
  • Cases and scenarios drawn from your own markets and products
  • Bilingual delivery in English and Arabic, in person or online
  • Scoring thresholds set with you — benchmarked to the roles you are hiring or developing for
