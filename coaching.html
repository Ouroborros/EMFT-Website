Coaching & Technical Mentoring

Development that continues after the classroom.

A program ends. A career does not. Coaching and mentoring carry the learning into the actual job — one person at a time, with a practitioner who has done the work.

One-to-one

Executive Coaching

For leaders stepping up — new mandates, bigger teams, harder decisions. Confidential, structured and focused entirely on the goals you set at the start.

  • First-time and newly promoted managers finding their footing
  • Senior leaders preparing for a broader or unfamiliar mandate
  • Executives navigating transformation, restructuring or merger
  • High-potential talent identified for the next generation of leadership
  • Team coaching where the challenge sits between people, not within one
Practitioner-led

Technical Mentoring

For specialists building depth. A mentor who works in the field guides the individual through real problems from their own desk — not a case study.

  • Credit, risk, treasury and investment analysts building judgement
  • Cybersecurity and data specialists working toward certification
  • Graduates moving from a training cohort into a live role
  • Candidates preparing for CFA, CISI and other qualification exams
  • Newly appointed supervisors and inspectors at regulators
Why one-to-one

Some capability cannot be built in a room of thirty.

01

Where an assessment has been run, coaching starts from what it found — the gap is known before the first session, not guessed at.

02

Matched, Not Assigned

How it works

Four steps, then the work is theirs.

Engagements are usually run over three to six months, in person or online, in English or Arabic. Length and cadence are set with you.

01

Agree the Goal

A three-way conversation between the participant, their sponsor and us — so everyone is working toward the same outcome.

02

Match

We propose a coach or mentor, and the participant meets them before anything is committed. The fit has to be right.

03

Work the Sessions

Regular one-to-one sessions on real material, with practice between them. What is said in the room stays in the room.

04

Review

A close-out against the original goal, with a development plan the participant and their manager can keep using.

Who are you developing?

Tell us the person, the role and where you want them to be — we will propose the coach or mentor.
