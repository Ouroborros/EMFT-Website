Privacy Policy

This policy covers this website and the enquiries sent through it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

Last updated 2 August 2026

Who we are

Emerging Market Financial Training (“EMFT”, “we”) is a training and capability-development firm headquartered in London, with offices in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Riyadh. For any question about this policy, write to info@emergingmarketft.com

What this website stores in your browser

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies, and runs no third-party analytics. It stores two items in your browser’s local storage, both of which stay on your device:

  • Language preference — whether you chose English or Arabic, so the choice survives when you move between pages.
  • Privacy notice acknowledgement — a flag recording that you dismissed the notice, so it is not shown again.

Clearing your browser’s site data removes both. The site works without either.

Web fonts are requested from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google’s servers and Google receives your IP address as part of that request. The Learning Portal is a separate system with its own sign-in and its own privacy terms.

What we collect when you contact us

If you send an enquiry through the contact form, or email or call us directly, we receive what you choose to give us: your name, job title, organization, work email, telephone number, country, area of interest, expected participant numbers, preferred delivery period and your message.

Why we hold it, and for how long

We use those details for one purpose: to answer your enquiry and, if it goes further, to scope and deliver the work. We do not sell them, and we do not add you to a marketing list on the strength of an enquiry alone.

Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted within twenty-four months. Where an engagement follows, records are kept for as long as the contract and the relevant tax and accounting rules require.

Who else sees it

Enquiry details are seen by EMFT staff working on your request, and by the email and business systems we use to run the firm. Where a trainer or assessor needs context to prepare, they are told what the work requires and no more. We do not pass your details to anyone else without your instruction, unless the law requires it.

Where it is held

EMFT operates across the United Kingdom, the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia, so your details may be handled in any of those places. Wherever they sit, the protections described here apply.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. Write to info@emergingmarketft.com and we will respond within thirty days. If you are in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area and are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to your data protection regulator.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Material changes will be summarized at the top of this page.
