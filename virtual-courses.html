Virtual Courses

Open courses, run live online.

Scheduled sessions you can join as an individual or a small team, taught live by the same practitioners who deliver our in-house programs, in English or Arabic. Dates appear here as they are confirmed.

How a virtual course runs

01

Live, not recorded

Every session is taught in real time, so you can ask questions and work through cases with the trainer rather than watch a video.

02

Practitioner-led

The same trainers who deliver our programs inside banks, central banks, sovereign wealth funds and regulators.

03

Short daily windows

Sessions run in half-day blocks, so a course fits around the working week instead of replacing it.

04

Materials and certificate

Slides, references and exercises come through the Learning Portal, with a certificate of completion at the end.
