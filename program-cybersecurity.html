Program · Two tracks

Cybersecurity — Defensive & Offensive Tracks

Two lab-based tracks that build job-ready security capability: a defensive track running from security fundamentals to Security+, ISO 27001 and Certified CyberDefender, and an offensive track taking penetration testers to eCPPT.

At a glance

Duration

3–12 months per track, cohort-based

Level

Foundation to certification

Delivery

In person, virtual or blended

Languages

English and Arabic

Certifications

CompTIA Security+ · ISO/IEC 27001 · Certified CyberDefender (CCD) · eCPPT (INE)

Who attends

Built for the people doing the job

  • Graduates entering SOC, incident response and security operations roles
  • IT staff moving into dedicated security positions
  • Institutions building an internal penetration-testing capability
  • Banks and regulators standing up or upskilling a cyber team
What you will achieve

Outcomes you can put to work

  • Defend: monitor, detect and respond to incidents in a live lab environment
  • Attack: run structured penetration tests and report findings professionally
  • Pass the certification each track targets — our cohorts train to the exam
  • Apply banking-specific threat scenarios, not generic IT examples
  • Screen and select candidates objectively when building a cohort
Program outline

Indicative outline — customized before delivery

Nothing runs as written here. Content, cases and duration are scoped against your objectives, your products and your market before a single session is delivered.

01

Security foundations

Networks, systems and the attacker's view — the ground both tracks stand on.

02

Defensive track: SOC operations

Monitoring, SIEM, triage and incident response, hands-on.

03

Defensive track: standards

Security+ and ISO/IEC 27001 — controls, audits and the certification exams.

04

Offensive track: methodology

Reconnaissance to reporting — the discipline of a professional engagement.

05

Offensive track: eCPPT labs

Guided exploitation labs building to the practical certification exam.

06

Capstone exercise

Blue and red teams face each other in a closing scenario built on your environment.
